## Holes

1. **Deposits leave no vault event.** The vault logs `Borrowed`, `Repaid`, `Withdrawn` and line updates, but the admin's three deposits (6 USDG) appear only as USDG `Transfer`s. Anyone rebuilding the books from the vault's own log is 6 USDG short. The books only close by also reading USDG transfers. *Fix: emit a `Deposited` event, or fund through a function that logs.*
2. **Lines follow the agent ID, not the agent's holder.** A line is keyed to an ERC-8004 agent ID, and the right to borrow belongs to whoever controls that ID (`isController`: the owner, or a delegate set with `setDelegate`). Line #8811 is open at 5 USDG. If agent #8811 is sold, the buyer can draw the 5 USDG that was extended to Bucks. Whether a delegate set by the previous owner survives that sale can't be checked, because the vault's source isn't verified (see 3). *Worth deciding on purpose: re-underwrite on a change of holder, or not.*
3. **The source isn't verified** (no Sourcify match), so this audit reads behaviour and the public function list, not the code.
4. **Lines exceed cash by 1 USDG.** Open lines total 6 USDG (1 + 5) against 5 USDG in the vault. If both are drawn at once, the second draw fails. The admin can also withdraw at any time, as on 11-14, so an open line isn't a promise of cash.

## Verdict

**Every loan was repaid on time and in full, and the cash reconciles to the chain to the cent.** The holes are about what an outside reader can *see* and what happens *later* (1, 2, 4), not about money gone missing. Agent_Minimum's books match the chain.
