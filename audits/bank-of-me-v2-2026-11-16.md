## Your three questions (fork tests)

**1. Can a line survive a wallet change? No.**
- Agent #8811 borrowed and repaid 1 USDG from `0xf585…`, then the ID was transferred to a new holder.
- The registry cleared the agent wallet.
- The seller's next `borrow` reverts with `agent wallet changed: line dead`; the buyer's reverts with `not the line's wallet`.
- Buying a used agent ID doesn't buy its line, and selling it kills the seller's line.

**2. Can lines ever exceed cash? No.**
- `setLine` over the cash reverts with `lines exceed cash`.
- `withdraw` that would leave limits uncovered reverts with `would leave lines uncovered`.

**3. Is the counter carry-over worse than you think? Not as recorded.**
- After one borrow and repay, `lines(8811)` reads `[0xf585…, 5 USDG, 0, 1, 1]`.
- After the sale it still names `0xf585…` next to those counts, so a reader can tell who earned them.
- **Untested:** whether a *new* line for the same agent and a new wallet starts at 0/0 or inherits 1/1. The hole below blocked the test. Reset the counters whenever the line's wallet changes.

## New hole: a dead line freezes the vault's cash
- After the sale, the owner can't touch line #8811: `setLine(8811, …)` reverts with `no agent wallet`, because the registry cleared it on transfer.
- That dead line still counts in `totalLimits`, so its 5 USDG can't be withdrawn (`would leave lines uncovered`) or lent to anyone else (`lines exceed cash`).
- That lasts until the new holder of #8811 sets an agent wallet. A buyer who never does can keep the lender's cash locked indefinitely.
- **Fix:** let the owner close a line to 0 without the wallet check, or stop counting a dead line (wallet ≠ the registry's current agent wallet) toward `totalLimits`.
